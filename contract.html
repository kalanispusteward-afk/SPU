Article 1 Absence from Work ▼

Employees must report any absence to their manager at least 3 hours before the scheduled start of their shift, unless an emergency makes this impossible.

Doctor's Certificate

After 3 or more consecutive missed shifts due to illness, the Hospital may require a physician's certificate confirming the illness, dates of care, and expected return date. Employees have 7 days to obtain and produce such a certificate if requested.

Fitness for Duty

The Hospital may require a fitness-for-duty exam when there is a good-faith question about an employee's ability to safely return to work. If removed pending such an exam, the employee is placed on paid administrative leave at straight time — unless the delay is caused by the employee or the exam shows the employee is not fit for duty.

Article 3 Attendance ▼

Occurrences

One unscheduled absence = 1 occurrence (if called in properly at least 3 hours before shift). Leaving before completing half your shift without prior approval = 1 occurrence. Tardiness (over 5 minutes late) = ½ occurrence. Failing to call in properly adds an extra occurrence.

No Call / No Show

Failing to notify the supervisor before the shift AND failing to clock in within the first 2 hours = 3 occurrences. Two no call/no shows in a 12-month period = termination.

Discipline Ladder (rolling 12-month period)

  • 5 occurrences = Coaching session
  • 6 occurrences = First written warning
  • 7 occurrences = Final written warning
  • 8 occurrences = 2-day unpaid suspension
  • 9 occurrences = Termination (just cause)
  • 12 instances of tardiness in 12 months = Termination

Excused Absences (do NOT count as occurrences)

Approved leaves of absence, on-the-job injuries (workers' comp), emergency medical procedures, jury duty, approved shift trades, bereavement leave.

Holiday Enhanced Occurrences

Absences on or immediately before/after recognized holidays may result in enhanced occurrences — up to 4 total for holiday-related incidents, 3 for other designated high call-out days.

Article 4 Bereavement Leave ▼

If a death occurs in an employee's immediate family, paid time off is available for up to 24 hours for full-time employees and 12 hours for part-time employees within 30 days following the death.

Immediate Family

Spouse, parent, step-parent, legal guardian, grandparents, grandchildren, mother/father-in-law, children, step-children, legal ward, brother/sister (including in-law), son/daughter-in-law, domestic partner and their relatives. Miscarriage, stillbirth, or similar outcome is also covered.

Bereavement pay is at base hourly wage with no premium pay and does not count as hours worked. Additional bereavement days may be requested using PTO.

Article 5 Call Off Procedure ▼

Call Off Order (by seniority within each group)

  • 1. Agency and Travelers
  • 2. VSP on overtime or other premium pay
  • 3. Part-time on overtime or other premium pay
  • 4. Full-time on overtime or other premium pay
  • 5. VSP
  • 6. Solicited volunteers
  • 7. Part-time and full-time

Your Options When Called Off

You may use voluntary unpaid leave or PTO — your choice. Once called off, you are NOT required to maintain contact or remain available for the rest of the shift.

Employees called back from call off will be called in the reverse of the above order.

Article 9 Disciplinary Action ▼

The Hospital may suspend, discharge, or otherwise discipline an employee for just cause only.

Key Protections

  • Any written warning, suspension, or discharge of a non-probationary employee is grievable.
  • Coaching sessions (documented or not) are NOT considered discipline and are NOT grievable — but can be part of a grievance over higher-level discipline for the same conduct.
  • Disciplinary actions generally expire after 12 months. Final written warnings and unpaid suspensions may be used for up to 2 years for similar conduct.
  • Sexual harassment incidents have no expiration and may be considered in future discipline.
  • If discipline is overturned through arbitration or conceded by the Hospital, all references must be purged from your personnel file.

Right to Representation

You may have a Union Steward present when questioned about potential discipline or when discipline is being issued. If your preferred steward is unavailable, you have up to 1 hour to find an alternate.

Article 12 Grievance and Arbitration ▼

What Is a Grievance?

A dispute or disagreement about the interpretation, application, or compliance with specific provisions of the CBA, or whether discipline/discharge was for just cause.

Critical Time Limits (weekends and holidays excluded)

  • Step 1: Must be filed within 15 days of the event (or when you knew/should have known). Meeting within 10 days, written response within 15 days.
  • Step 2: Appeal within 15 days of receiving Step 1 response. Meeting within 15 days, written response within 15 days.
  • Step 3 (Arbitration): Appeal within 30 days of Step 2 response.

Missing a deadline voids the grievance permanently. Management missing a deadline does NOT kill your grievance — you may still advance to the next step.

Grievance Form

Grievances must be submitted on the official Grievance Form (Appendix B). Failure to include all information does not automatically preclude the grievance, but the Hospital must notify the union of missing info in writing.

Article 14 Holidays ▼

Recognized Holidays

  • New Year's Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Independence Day (July 4)
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Christmas Day

Holiday Worked Premium

Employees required to work on a recognized holiday receive time and one-half their base hourly rate for hours worked. Holiday hours run from 11:00 PM the day before the holiday through 11:00 PM on the day of the holiday. Must work at least 4 consecutive hours during holiday hours to qualify.

If the holiday falls on Saturday, the observed holiday for Mon–Fri departments is Friday. If it falls on Sunday, the observed holiday is Monday.

Article 15 Hours of Work and Overtime ▼

Breaks and Meal Periods

Employees on shifts of at least 8 hours receive one 30-minute unpaid meal period and two 15-minute paid rest breaks. If required to stay at your workstation during a meal period, you remain in paid status. An additional paid rest break is granted if you work 4+ hours beyond your regular shift.

Consecutive Hours

An employee who works more than 16 consecutive hours must have at least 8 hours off before returning to work on the regular schedule.

Reporting Pay

If you report to work without prior notice that no work is available, you are guaranteed at least 2 hours of work or pay at your regular rate.

Travel Time

If directed to travel to another Hospital location during your shift, you are paid for reasonable travel time.

Article 18 Jury / Court Duty ▼

Full-time and part-time employees are paid their base hourly rate for time missed due to required jury duty. Show your supervisor your summons upon receipt.

If not selected for jury duty or released after less than 4 hours, you must return to work for the remainder of your shift. Night shift employees required to appear for jury duty are released from their shift the night before the appearance if requested.

Time spent on court appearances at the Hospital's request (depositions, interviews, testimony) is paid at base rate and counts as hours worked for overtime purposes.

Article 19 Labor-Management Committee ▼

The Labor-Management Committee (LMC) meets quarterly (at the union's request) to review and discuss issues of mutual concern, including staffing concerns. Meetings may not exceed 2 hours unless jointly agreed.

The committee includes up to 3 employer representatives and 3 union employee representatives, plus 1 HR representative and 1 Union Staff Representative. The LMC will not discuss disciplinary matters, pending grievances, or contract negotiation issues. It is the exclusive means to address staffing concerns.

Employees on the committee are scheduled off and paid at their base rate for time spent attending meetings, counted as hours worked for overtime.

Meetings must be scheduled at least 60 days in advance with a jointly agreed agenda.

Article 20 Layoff / Recall ▼

The Hospital must make every reasonable effort to avoid layoffs, using attrition, retraining, and job matching first. At least 2 weeks notice (or 2 weeks pay) must be given before layoff.

Layoff Order

Temporaries and travelers first, then least senior employees in the affected classification/department/shift. Laid-off employees may bump less senior employees in their classification with up to 10 days of orientation.

Severance Pay

  • 91 days–3 years: 2 weeks
  • 3–5 years: 3 weeks
  • 5–8 years: 4 weeks
  • 8–10 years: 5 weeks
  • 10–15 years: 8 weeks
  • 15–20 years: 10 weeks
  • 20+ years: 12 weeks

Recall

Employees remain on the recall roster for 12 months (24 months for employees with 5+ years of service). Recall is in inverse order of seniority. You must accept recall within 2 business days and return to work within 14 days of notification.

Article 25 Mandatory Overtime ▼

Mandatory overtime is a last resort. The Hospital must first exhaust alternatives including volunteers, managers, agency/travelers before requiring mandatory overtime.

Key Protections

  • No employee may be required to work more than 16 consecutive hours.
  • If you volunteer to work beyond 16 hours, you may request to delay your next shift start by up to 8 hours — the Hospital cannot unreasonably deny this.
  • All mandatory overtime hours are paid at double time, regardless of whether you have reached 40 hours in the workweek.
Article 26 Miscellaneous Benefits ▼
  • Free Parking: The Hospital must provide parking at no charge.
  • Cafeteria: Free coffee and at least 20% discount off visitor prices.
  • Hospital Services: Employee discount on hospital services at Sunrise.
  • HCA Programs: Banking services, Day Care Spending Account, Medical/Vision Spending Account, HCA Scholarship Program, Adoption Assistance, Direct Deposit, Student Loan Repayment, Tuition Reimbursement, Child Care Subsidy, Employee Stock Purchase Plan, Certification Support.
Article 29 Non-Discrimination ▼

Neither the Hospital nor the Union shall discriminate against any employee on account of race, color, religion, national origin, age, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, genetic information, protected veteran status, disability, union activity, or union membership.

Alleged violations are subject to the grievance procedure (Article 12), but will not be referred to arbitration. Employees retain the right to seek relief from outside the grievance process, including courts and administrative agencies.

Article 30 Paid Time Off (PTO) ▼

Full-Time Accrual Rates

  • 0–4 years: 184 hours/year (7.08 hrs/pay period), max 276 hours
  • 5–9 years: 223 hours/year (8.60 hrs/pay period), max 336 hours
  • 10+ years: 263 hours/year (10.15 hrs/pay period), max 396 hours

Cash Out

You may cash out PTO over 80 hours twice per year at $0.90 on the dollar. Minimum cash-out is 8 hours. If you leave or reduce to VSP, all PTO is paid out at 100%.

Scheduling — Jan–Jun

Submit requests September 1–30 of the prior year. Seniority-based priority in three rounds, then first-come first-served after the deadline.

Scheduling — Jul–Dec

Submit requests March 1–31 of the current year. Same seniority-based round system applies.

PTO Donation

Employees may donate PTO (minimum 1 hour) to a coworker experiencing hardship due to illness/injury of themselves or an immediate family member. Donor must retain at least 40 hours after the donation and may not donate more than 50% of their balance.

Article 32 Pay Check Errors ▼

Underpayment errors must be corrected and an additional check issued no later than the next paycheck distributed 5 or more days after the Hospital is notified of the error via a paycheck discrepancy form or other written method.

Article 33 Personnel Files ▼

Employees have the right to inspect electronic copies of their personnel file through the HR Answers portal and to make their own copies at no charge using the computer in Human Resources.

If you dispute the accuracy of materials in your file, you may submit a response which will be attached to the disputed materials. Union representatives may inspect records in connection with a potential or pending grievance, if authorized in writing by the grievant.

Article 37 Safety and Health ▼

The Hospital must comply with all state and federal workplace safety laws. Employees cannot be required to work in conditions that are hazardous in violation of law.

Retaliation Prohibited

No employee will be disciplined or retaliated against for reporting unsafe conditions. If the Hospital fails to remedy a safety issue within a reasonable time, the employee or union may contact appropriate state or federal agencies.

Free Vaccinations and Testing Provided

Hepatitis B & C testing (following exposure), titer testing, vaccines for measles/mumps/chicken pox/rubella/rubeola, TB screening, HIV testing (following exposure), flu vaccines, COVID-19 vaccine, and any other vaccination required by the Hospital — all at no cost to employees.

Harassment

The Hospital does not tolerate unlawful harassment or threats of physical harm. Complaints should first go to your immediate supervisor. If unresolved, submit in writing to the Director of Labor Relations within 14 calendar days of the occurrence.

Article 39 Scheduling ▼

Schedules covering at least 4 weeks must normally be posted at least 13 days in advance. Employees may submit written requests for days off. Conflicting requests are resolved by rotating seniority.

Weekends

Full-time day shift employees: the Hospital will make every reasonable effort not to require more than 4 weekend shifts per 4-week schedule. Full-time night shift: not more than 6 weekend shifts per 4-week schedule. Part-time employees must work at least 2 weekend days or 3 weekend night shifts per 4-week schedule.

Schedule Changes After Posting

The Hospital must first seek mutual agreement. If no agreement, shift rotation is applied equitably starting with the least senior qualified employee.

Article 40 Seniority ▼

Hospital-wide seniority: unbroken length of service since your most recent hire into a full-time or part-time position.

Classification seniority: unbroken length of service within your current classification as full-time or part-time.

Seniority Terminates Upon

  • Resignation (unless returning within 90 days)
  • Discharge for just cause
  • Layoff for 12 months (less than 5 years service) or 24 months (5+ years service)

During the first 18 months after a voluntary classification/department change, classification seniority applies for vacation requests, holiday rotation, and shift bidding. After 18 months, hospital seniority applies for all purposes.

Article 42 Shop Stewards ▼

Weingarten Rights (Section 42.5)

In any investigatory interview you reasonably believe could result in discipline, you have the right to request a Shop Steward or field representative. If a steward will not be available within 24 hours, the interview may proceed without further delay.

What to say: "I request union representation before this meeting proceeds."

Steward Duties

Stewards inform members of their rights, ensure the contract is being observed, and investigate and assist in processing grievances. If stewards attend grievance meetings or investigate during working time, they must seek permission from the Hospital (which cannot be arbitrarily denied). Stewards do not lose pay for attending grievance meetings or investigations.

Training and Leave

Upon advance written request, stewards may receive up to 5 days without pay per calendar year for union educational programs, provided coverage can be obtained without premium pay.

Article 45 Status of Employment ▼

Full-Time

Scheduled to work a minimum of 64 hours per pay period on a regular basis in a non-relief, non-temporary capacity.

Part-Time

Scheduled to work at least 40 but less than 64 hours per pay period on a regular basis.

VSP (Variable Staffing Pool)

Variable hours based on Hospital needs. Must work minimum 3 shifts per schedule (if desired), 1 weekend shift/month, 1 major holiday (Thanksgiving, Christmas, or New Year's), and 1 minor holiday per year as needed. VSP employees are ineligible for most fringe benefits.

Reclassification

A VSP employee working a regular schedule of 40+ hours/pay period for 12 of 15 consecutive pay periods may request reclassification to part-time. A part-time or VSP working 64+ hours/pay period may request reclassification to full-time.

Article 47 Time Off to Vote ▼

If your work schedule does not give you sufficient time to vote in a federal, state, county, or city election, the Hospital will adjust your hours or give you paid time off to vote. Request this in advance so your manager can arrange coverage.

Article 52 Wages ▼

Wage Implementation Schedule

  • Year 1: First full pay period after ratification (October 2025) — employees brought up to target step or receive up to 7% increase, whichever is less.
  • Year 2: First full pay period of July 2026 — same process applied to Year 2 scales.
  • Year 3: First full pay period of July 2027 — same process applied to Year 3 scales.

Employees whose rate is at or within 2% of the applicable step receive a 2% increase plus the remainder as a lump sum bonus.

Grade Classifications

  • S1: Social Worker BSW
  • S2: Social Worker MSW
  • S3: Medical Technologist
  • S4: Senior Medical Technologist
  • S5: Occupational Therapist, Physical Therapist, Speech Language Pathologist
  • S6: Clinical Pharmacist

Clinical Pharmacists working in the ED receive an additional 5% hourly differential for all ED hours worked.

Article 53 Weekend and Shift Differentials ▼

Evening Shift Differential — $1.50/hr

Shifts scheduled 3:00 PM – 11:00 PM. Must work at least 4 consecutive hours to qualify.

Night Shift Differential — $2.10/hr (full-time/part-time) / $1.50/hr (VSP)

Shifts scheduled 7:00 PM – 7:00 AM or 11:00 PM – 7:00 AM. Must work at least 4 consecutive hours.

Weekend Shift Differential — $1.50/hr

Shifts commencing on or after 11:00 PM Friday and ending on or before 11:00 PM Sunday. Must work at least 4 consecutive hours.

Evening and night differentials may be combined with weekend differential. All three may be combined with holiday differentials. Differentials apply only to productive hours worked.

Article 55 Duration ▼

This Agreement is effective October 3, 2025 and continues in effect through June 30, 2028. It automatically renews year to year unless either party gives written notice of intent to terminate or amend at least 90 days before the expiration date.
