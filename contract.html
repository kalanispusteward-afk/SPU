Ask The Contract

Guides for the Professional Bargaining Unit Agreement[cite: 7].

Absence Notification

Employees must report absences to management at least three (3) hours prior to their shift start[cite: 28].

Attendance Discipline

Discipline levels for occurrences in a rolling 12-month period include [cite: 86-91]:

  • 6 Occurrences: First Written Warning
  • 7 Occurrences: Final Written Warning
  • 8 Occurrences: Two-day unpaid suspension
  • 9 Occurrences: Just cause for termination

Mandatory Overtime

Mandatory overtime is paid at Double Time, regardless of total hours worked in the week[cite: 553]. No employee is required to work more than 16 consecutive hours[cite: 549].
