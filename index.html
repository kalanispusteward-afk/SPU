Our Mission The mission of the Sunrise Professionals Unit is to advocate for the highest level of patient care, equitable compensation, and safe working conditions for all members. We are committed to promoting a Just Culture — a framework championed by The Joint Commission that balances accountability with learning, encourages the reporting of errors and concerns without fear of retaliation, and fosters an environment where every voice is heard and every right is defended.

Know Your Rights

Open Grievance
Categories

The following categories represent types of grievances currently being monitored and processed by your stewards. Specific case details are kept confidential to protect members.

Active
Disciplinary Action

Disputes related to written warnings, suspensions, or terminations that members believe were issued unfairly or without just cause under our contract.

Active
Contract Violations

Cases where management has failed to follow the terms of our collective bargaining agreement including scheduling, pay rates, or work assignments.

Active
Workplace Safety

Concerns regarding unsafe working conditions, failure to provide proper equipment, or inadequate responses to reported hazards and safety issues.

Active
Discrimination & Harassment

Issues involving unfair treatment, hostile work environment, or conduct that violates member rights under law or our collective bargaining agreement.

Active
Pay & Benefits

Discrepancies in wages, improper denial of benefits, overtime disputes, or failure to apply negotiated raises and step increases correctly.

Monitoring
Workload & Staffing

Situations where excessive workloads, understaffing, or mandatory overtime may violate contract provisions or create unsafe working conditions.

Your Contract — Plain English

Know Your
Rights

Every right below comes directly from your CBA with Sunrise Hospital, effective October 3, 2025 through June 30, 2028.

⚠

Weingarten Rights

Article 42.5 & Article 9

If you are called into a meeting you believe could result in discipline — STOP. Request a union steward before and during that meeting.
  • You have the right to a Shop Steward during any investigatory interview you believe may result in discipline.
  • You also have the right to a steward when discipline is actually being issued.
  • If your preferred steward is unavailable, you get up to one hour to find an alternate.
  • Management cannot proceed without delay for more than one hour if a steward is not available.
Say this: "I request union representation before this meeting proceeds."
📄

Grievance Deadlines

Article 12

15
Days to file Step 1 from incident
15
Days to appeal to Step 2
30
Days to appeal to arbitration
  • Missing a deadline voids the grievance permanently. Never wait.
  • Management missing a deadline does NOT kill your grievance.
  • Weekends and holidays do not count toward time limits.
  • The union can file a grievance on behalf of one or more members.
When in doubt, file early. You can withdraw — you cannot un-miss a deadline.
🚫

Disciplinary Action

Article 9

  • Management may only discipline or discharge you for just cause.
  • Any written warning, suspension, or discharge of a non-probationary employee can be grieved.
  • Coaching sessions are NOT grievable on their own — but can be grieved as part of higher-level discipline.
  • Most discipline expires after 12 months. Final written warnings and suspensions may be used for up to 2 years.
  • If discipline is overturned, all references must be removed from your personnel file.
🕑

Attendance & Occurrences

Article 3

  • One unscheduled absence = one occurrence if you called in at least 3 hours before your shift.
  • Arriving more than 5 minutes late = one-half occurrence.
  • Failing to call in properly adds an extra occurrence on top of the absence.
  • No call/no show = 3 occurrences. Two in 12 months = termination.
  • Discipline ladder (rolling 12 months): 5 = coaching, 6 = 1st warning, 7 = final warning, 8 = 2-day suspension, 9 = termination.
  • Approved leaves, on-the-job injuries, jury duty, and bereavement do NOT count as occurrences.
Always call your supervisor — not a coworker — at least 3 hours before your shift.
💰

Mandatory Overtime

Article 25

  • Mandatory overtime is a last resort — management must first try volunteers, agency, and travelers.
  • No employee can be required to work more than 16 consecutive hours.
  • If you work beyond 16 hours voluntarily, you may request to delay your next shift start by up to 8 hours.
  • All mandatory overtime hours are paid at double time — regardless of whether you have hit 40 hours.
🌞

Call-Off Procedure

Article 5

  • Call-off order: (1) Agency/travelers, (2) VSP on OT, (3) Part-time on OT, (4) Full-time on OT, (5) VSP, (6) Volunteers, (7) Part-time and full-time by seniority.
  • If called off, you may use PTO or voluntary unpaid leave — your choice.
  • Once called off, you are NOT required to stay available for the rest of the shift.
🏠

PTO & Scheduling

Article 30

  • Full-time accrual: 184 hrs/yr (0–4 yrs), 223 hrs/yr (5–9 yrs), 263 hrs/yr (10+ yrs).
  • Jan–Jun PTO: submit requests September 1–30 of prior year.
  • Jul–Dec PTO: submit requests March 1–31. Seniority determines priority.
  • You can cash out PTO over 80 hours twice per year at 90 cents on the dollar.
  • If you leave or reduce to VSP, all accrued PTO pays out at 100%.
📈

Wages & Differentials

Articles 52 & 53

  • Year 2 wage scales take effect the first full pay period of July 2026.
  • Paycheck errors must be corrected no later than the next paycheck 5+ days after notification.
  • Evening differential: $1.50/hr (shifts 3–11 PM, minimum 4 consecutive hours).
  • Night differential: $2.10/hr full-time/part-time (shifts 7 PM–7 AM or 11 PM–7 AM).
  • Weekend differential: $1.50/hr (Friday 11 PM through Sunday 11 PM).
  • Holiday worked: time and one-half for recognized holidays (minimum 4 consecutive hours).
  • Charge/relief supervisor differential: 5% per hour for MTs, Pharmacists, OTs, PTs, SLPs.
🎓

Benefits You May Not Know

Articles 13, 26

  • Free parking — the hospital must provide it at no charge.
  • Free coffee and at least 20% cafeteria discount off visitor prices.
  • Free medical malpractice insurance from your first day.
  • Full-time employees and dependents: Aetna POS at no cost. Part-time with 3+ years also free.
  • HCA offers: 401(k) match, tuition reimbursement, student loan repayment, adoption assistance, childcare subsidy.
  • Bereavement: up to 24 hours paid (full-time) or 12 hours (part-time) within 30 days of a death in your immediate family.
  • You have the right to paid time off to vote if your schedule prevents it.
